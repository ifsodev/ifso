## Notes
1. **The live code is newer than the last graded commit.** The full report (8.9) is on `4318745`. The build of record
   `2cd783d` adds two contract fixes on top of it (`0d92659` M-1 self-dispute bond split, `e85a41e` I-16 strict
   acceptBy < closeTime < earliestResolve; 2 files in `contracts/src`). `findings.tsv` still lists M-1 as open. A
   `rescore` on main is needed to close M-1 against the deployed code; this verify does not change the grade.
2. **Testnet signer sets.** The owner Safe and the panel Safe have the same three owners, and the deployer is one of
   them. This is recorded as testnet-only in the deployment notes. PostDeployCheck compares the two Safe addresses,
   not their owner lists, so it would not catch the same overlap on mainnet. The mainnet verify runbook checks it by
   hand; adding an owner-set comparison (and "deployer is not a Safe owner") to `_assertDeployment` would make it a
   gate.

An AI audit grade is a strong pre-audit signal, not a substitute for a human audit before large TVL.
